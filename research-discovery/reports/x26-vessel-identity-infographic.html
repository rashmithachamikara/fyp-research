05 / What would count as progress?

Measure the trade-off, not just the group count.

FragmentationHow often one vessel is split.
False mergesHow often distinct vessels mix.
CoverageHow many observations receive a decision.
Decision delayHow many observations pass before deciding.

Use held-out identities, duplicate-safe splits and varied arrival orders. Report grouping quality with B-cubed F1 and ARI. Keep uncertainty from observation order separate from evidence across vessels.

Before choosing this topic: verify the distinction from existing prototype and split/merge methods; reconcile 88 reported query tracks with 176 filename ID labels in the inspected HOSS release; audit checkpoint training identities and reproduce the comparison.

HOSS-Bench further trained its DINOv3 baseline. Generic cached features would be a different baseline. Controlled image orders do not demonstrate chronological voyage tracking.

Claim boundary: consistent visual identity does not establish a vessel’s registration, intentional AIS shutdown or illegal activity. No proposed-method performance has been measured.
