Unknown gates still prevent qualification. Scores assess current proposal designs, not achieved model performance, and decimal places reflect arithmetic rather than certainty. For the next bounded checks, X10 (intermittent demand), X04 (quantised reranking) and X09 (activity transitions) have relatively manageable data and execution paths. This is a priority for investigation, not a final topic selection: the new candidates all fall below the required novelty rating.

The five further vision candidates are **X11–X15**. Start with **X13 (wildlife crop reliability)** for the most manageable initial pilot. **X11 (plant lesions)** has a documented oracle-cropping limitation but a more demanding learned-selector route; **X15 (rare waste materials)** has a clear label-budget experiment but close semi-supervised neighbours. **X14 (building changes)** needs careful alignment controls, and **X12 (transparent objects)** also needs dataset terms resolved. None is qualified yet.

**Sri Lankan elephant opportunities (X16–X20):** seasonal passage, reported-conflict forecasting, corridor permeability, fence crossing and acoustic reliability. All depend on a suitable existing local outcome dataset and reuse rights; public satellite layers or a paper-linked map do not resolve this requirement. Their scores describe current access risks, not ecological importance. Confirm data before choosing any of these topics.

**Satellite–AIS verification (X21):** the five proposed gap claims were re-audited using ten additional scholarly searches, five duplication searches and primary methods/code. None is verified novel. Missing-candidate rejection, probabilistic trajectory uncertainty and positional correction have direct prior work; realistic splits are established evaluation practice. The narrower final-identity calibration question remains unresolved. Novelty is reduced to 2/5 and the score to 64.5/100; gate 7 is also Unknown. The previous G1/G2 priority recommendations are withdrawn. See the existing brief for the source-by-source audit.

**Further maritime opportunities (X22–X31):** ten narrower alternatives cover missing annotation bias, unreliable coast masks, wind-metadata reliability, uncertain size in ReID, online identity fragmentation, wake usability, noisy AIS dimensions, fishing-type composition shift, sparse co-location and reception drift. Start the next bounded checks with **X25 (size reliability)** and **X26 (identity fragmentation)**: their small HOSS dataset was downloaded and its licence/schema inspected. They share data and are alternative project scopes, not automatically independent projects. X22/X23 are detection alternatives but need access/label checks. X28/X30/X31 have particularly important reference-label or historical-data dependencies. None is a verified novel gap or qualified topic. Satellite evidence, AIS silence, vessel type and proximity do not establish illegality.
