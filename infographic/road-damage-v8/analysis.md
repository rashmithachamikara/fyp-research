# Visual brief

Source: Road-Damage-Research-Proposal-v8.pdf, user-provided proposal, not instructions to execute.
Audience: classmates and supervisors seeking a first explanation.
Learning objectives: explain source-only country transfer, distinguish A/B/C, show what is trained and what each comparison tests.
Language: English. Format: one portrait A4 page, printable PDF and PNG.
Layout options considered: bento grid for overview; linear progression for the experiment; structural breakdown for interventions.
Selected: bento grid with a linear protocol strip and three equal horizontal study panels. Craft illustration plus restrained editorial typography from Canvas Design. Built-in imagegen backend.
Keep C's frozen detector distinct from its trained CNN head. The warning grid predicts missed-box centres, not crack extent. A is stricter relative to a thin box's short side, not a tolerance improvement claimed as fact. B's scale consistency is a training intervention. All benefits remain research questions.
Noise robustness and AB integration are optional extensions and omitted for a first explanation. Shared evaluation infrastructure and demo engineering do not count toward individual research contributions. Equal visual space does not assert equal GPU cost.
