# Field Notes

Clarity comes from a deliberate rhythm: a broad opening, a single directional experiment, three equally weighted interventions, and a compact evidence strip. The page should feel meticulously crafted, with a disciplined grid and generous space between ideas. Each visual carries a concept before its accompanying sentence is read.

Warm ivory paper and dark ink establish a quiet editorial setting. Blue, teal and amber distinguish three parallel lines of enquiry, with repeated letter markers supplying a second cue beyond colour. Fine road markings and bounding rectangles create a subtle common visual language. Colour serves navigation rather than decoration.

Typography uses a confident display face for the opening and a clear sans serif for essential labels. Headings, diagrams and captions share consistent edges. Painstaking attention to line breaks and optical alignment makes the composition readable at page size; the quality should suggest the work of an expert designer.

Small illustrated comparisons explain changes in geometry, scale and prediction. There is no ornamental chart and no numerical result masquerading as evidence. The final refinement removes clutter, strengthens alignment and preserves whitespace so that the whole page reads as one carefully composed explanatory note.
