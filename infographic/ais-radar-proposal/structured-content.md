# Content structure

Title: Teaching AI to Read Vessel Movements
Question: Can a model trained on AIS recognise behaviour from radar tracks?
Sensor comparison: AIS broadcasts boat position; radar observes boats from outside. Draw the same journey, clear versus noisy and fragmented.
Step 1: Learn from real AIS; rules supply movement labels.
Step 2: Compare training approaches: clean AIS, simulated errors, domain adaptation with real unlabelled radar.
Step 3: Test on unseen real radar; paired AIS supplies reference labels only; compare with simple rules.
Step 4: Report motion label and confidence; allow uncertainty.
Research question: Which training approach handles noisy, incomplete tracks best?
Scope note: AIS-equipped vessels; movement labels do not establish illegal activity.
