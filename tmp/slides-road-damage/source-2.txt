Proposal Evaluations - Guidelines
INSTRUCTIONS TO THE STUDENTS:
1. Purpose of the Proposal Presentation
The proposal presentation aims to evaluate the students’ ability to:
 Clearly identify and justify a research problem and gap
 Demonstrate understanding of existing literature
 Proposing a feasible design plan and solution
 Present a structured research plan with module breakdowns
 Defend the proposed study academically
2. Presentation Format
 Duration: 10 minutes (Presentation) + 10 minutes (Q&A)
 Presentation Time Breakdown: 02 minutes for project introduction/ background
explanation + 08 minutes for individual module/s briefing
 Mode: Physical (Venue/s will be informed in due course)
 Slides: Maximum 12–15 slides
 Language: English (formal academic style)
3. Common Mistakes to Avoid
 Vague or too broad topics
 No clear research gap
 Objectives not aligned with problem
 Too much theory, no research focus
 Reading directly from slides
 Overcrowded slides
 Poor time management
